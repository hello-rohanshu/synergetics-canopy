Net Diagram of Angles and Edges for Basic Equilibrium 48 LCD Triangle in VE-icosa Grid: This is a coded detail of the basic spherical triangle shown shaded in Fig. [[1130.00 Omnidirectional Typewriter#1132.01|1132.01B]] and at Fig. [[450.00 Great Circles of the Vector Equilibrium and Icosahedron#453.01|453.01]]. It is the key to the trigonometry tables for the spherical central angles, the spherical face angles, the planar edge lengths, and the planar face angles presented at Table [[1130.00 Omnidirectional Typewriter#1132.01|1132.01D]]. (The drawing shows the spherical phase: angle and edge ratios are given for both spherical and planar VE.)
