Duo-Tet Star Polyhedron Defines Vector Equilibrium Involvement Domain: The Duo-Tet star polyhedron that first appears in Fig. [[987.00 Multiplication Only by Division#987.242|987.242A]] is shown here within a vector equilibrium net. The complex also illustrates the eight Eighth-Octa that must be added to the eight triangular faces of the vector equilibrium to form the nucleated cube__the total complex of which functions as the vector equilibrium nuclear involvement domain. A closest-sphere-packing evolution of this same transformation (adding eight Eighth-Octa to the VE's six triangular faces) appears at Fig. [[410.00 Closest Packing of Spheres#415.17|415.17]]
